# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are local and regional musicians, bands, and performing artists looking for rehearsal space, studio recording, live performance support, or event production in Waalwijk.

## Product Purpose

De Mads is a brochure-style site for a music and dance center that helps visitors understand available services and quickly contact the team to request rehearsal, studio, event, or rental support.

## Positioning

A locally rooted music center that combines professional rehearsal and recording facilities with a gezellig muziekcafé and practical support for bands, artists, and event hosts.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive on desktop or mobile to browse service offerings, verify the venue's capabilities, and choose a contact path: phone, email, or form inquiry. The site functions as a single-page local landing experience.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static web implementation using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
- Existing content is Dutch and centered on the Waalwijk location.
- Primary calls to action are direct contact and section-based information links.
- The current project has no evidence of a booking system, sign-in flow, or multi-page application.
- The website is intended as a live marketing presence for De Mads, not a transactional platform.

## Brand Commitments

- Brand name: De Mads.
- Local identity: Muziek & Danscentrum Waalwijk.
- Tone should remain direct, approachable, and service-oriented.
- Preserve the existing dark background aesthetic with warm accent color and highlighted local music identity.

## Evidence on Hand

- `index.html` contains the existing landing page copy, service sections, contact links, and local branding.
- `css/style.css` defines the current visual system, typography, and responsive layout.
- `js/main.js` powers the page preloader, mobile navigation, and embedded social widget loading.
- Metadata and copy emphasize studio, rehearsal, café, rental, performances, and local contact details.

## Product Principles

- Keep local musicians and bands as the primary focus.
- Make service options and contact actions clear and easy to use.
- Preserve the incumbent single-page brochure structure and Dutch language.
- Support a welcoming, practical experience for prospective visitors.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Existing site uses a skip link, ARIA labels, and keyboard-friendly navigation.
- Maintain accessible interactions and focus management in any future refinements.
